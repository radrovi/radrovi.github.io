Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

This policy applies to all mobile applications published by radrovi on Google Play.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Our apps require no account, no sign-in, and operate without a backend server. All values you enter — including saved calculations and scenarios — are stored only in local storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our apps display advertisements through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects the following from your device:

  • Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable identifier used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention
  • Approximate location — derived from IP address, used for regional ad relevance and legal compliance
  • Device and app information — device model, OS version, and in-app ad interactions

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by radrovi, and is not linked to your identity. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites.

3. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent message is shown on first launch via Google's User Messaging Platform. You may change your choice at any time from the app's settings screen.

4. Your choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, at any time: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because all app data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. We hold no copy and therefore cannot access, export, or delete it on your behalf.
